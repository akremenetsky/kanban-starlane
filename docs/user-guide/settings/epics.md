# Epics

Cards of a board grouped under an epic card. How to make and use them:
[Epics](../guide/cards-and-lists.md#epics).

## Epics

On by default. Shows epics on cards (the epic's name on its cards, the progress on the epic
card) and the actions that edit them (card menu, quick action).

When off, epics are hidden but stay in the board file; switching back on shows them again.
