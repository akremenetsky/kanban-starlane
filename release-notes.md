Changes for Obsidian's community plugin review; boards and their files are not affected.

- **Requires Obsidian 1.8.7 or newer** (was 1.6.2).
- The date picker no longer adds `flatpickr` classes to Obsidian's elements or CSS rules to its
  stylesheet.
- *New note from card* and pasting images use current Obsidian APIs.
- Unused parts of the bundled date picker removed; dependencies updated past known advisories.

See [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/akremenetsky/kanban-starlane/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
