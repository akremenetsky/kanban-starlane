### Added
- **Card relations**: link cards of the same board — *blocks*, *relates to*, *duplicates*,
  *causes*, *depends on* — from the card menu (*Add relation*), the new *Add relation* quick
  action, or the relations popover. Each relation shows on both cards: a card blocked by an
  open card gets a red *Blocked* mark in its header and a dashed checkbox, and the footer shows
  how many relations a card has. Click it to see them, jump to a related card or remove a
  relation. A blocker that is done or archived no longer blocks. Relations are kept in a hidden
  block at the end of the board file, so card text does not change. They can be switched off in
  the new *Card relations* settings.
